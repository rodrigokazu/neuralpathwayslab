### Consulting & Expert Services

</div>

<div style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 30px;">

I take on a limited number of remote consulting engagements alongside my academic research. If your team needs specialist analysis capacity in single-cell genomics, spatial transcriptomics, or computational neuroscience, I can help — from a one-off pipeline build to ongoing analytical support.

</div>

<h4>Single-Cell & Multiome Pipeline Build</h4>
<p>End-to-end scRNA-seq or multiome analysis: from raw data to a reproducible, documented scanpy/SnapATAC2 pipeline running on your HPC. Built on experience with 788,330-nucleus ALS datasets.</p>
</div>

<div class="col-sm-6 clearfix">

</div>
