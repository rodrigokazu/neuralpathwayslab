<div style="margin-top: 50px; text-align:justify;">

We map ALS in human spinal cord and brain at single-cell resolution, without taking the tissue apart. We use spatial transcriptomics, single-cell multiomics and network science to find the cells and genes that drive motor neurone disease.

Dr Rodrigo Kazu Siqueira leads the lab at the [Sheffield Institute for Translational Neuroscience (SITraN)](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/sitran), University of Sheffield, with an [MND Association](https://www.mndassociation.org/research) fellowship and a partnership with the [Snyder Lab](https://snyderlab.stanford.edu) at Stanford. [We are looking for PhD students.]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/prospective-students/)

</div>
